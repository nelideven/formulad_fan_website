WHAT IS FORMULA DRIFT?

Formula Drift is the premier professional drifting series in the United States, founded in 2004. It brings together the best drivers, cars, and fans to celebrate the art of controlled oversteer. With events across the country, FD has grown into a global motorsport phenomenon that millions favored worldwide.

WHAT ABOUT US??

The Formula Drift Fan Project is a non-profit, open-source hub built by fans to showcase the sport of drifting. From the culture that fuels it, to the drivers who define it, and the events that keep the smoke rolling, this project we made shows Formula Drift in all its glory. Whether you're here to learn what drifting is, explore driver profiles, or relive recent battles, you're part of the community that cares (which we do too). We frequently update the site with new content, and you can contribute too, so check back often for the latest news and highlights from the world of Formula Drift.

A BRIEF HISTORY

Formula Drift was founded by Jim Liaw in 2004, from grassroots drifting events into a professional series. The first season featured 8 events and quickly gained popularity for its unique blend of motorsport and car culture. Over the years, FD has expanded to include multiple classes, international events, and a growing fanbase that celebrates the skill and style of drifting.
