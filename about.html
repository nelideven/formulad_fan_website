BEHIND THE SMOKE

THE FAN PROJECT

OUR MISSION

The Formula Drift Fan Project is a non-profit, open-source hub built by fans to showcase the sport of drifting. From the culture that fuels it, to the drivers who define it, and the events that keep the smoke rolling, this project documents Formula Drift in all its glory. Whether you're here to learn what drifting is, explore driver profiles, or relive recent battles, you're part of the community that cares. We frequently update the site with new content, so check back often for the latest news and highlights from the world of Formula Drift.

FORMULA DRIFT'S MISSION

Formula Drift's mission is to advance the sport of professional drifting by creating world-class competitions, engaging fans across the globe, and providing drivers with a platform to showcase their skill, style, and innovation. The series strives to grow drifting as a motorsport while staying true to its grassroots origins, ensuring that every event delivers excitement across millions of viewers.
