THE FAN PROJECT

OUR MISSION

The Formula Drift Fan Project is a non-profit, open-source hub built by fans to showcase the sport of drifting. Our mission is to create an engaging, informative, and visually appealing platform that celebrates the drivers, events, and culture of Formula Drift. We aim to provide fans with easy access to schedules, driver profiles, and event highlights, while fostering a sense of community and passion for the sport. By sharing our love for drifting, we hope to contribute to the growth and appreciation of this unique motorsport around the world.

FORMULA DRIFT'S MISSION

Formula Drift's mission is to advance the sport of professional drifting by creating world-class competitions, engaging fans across the globe, and providing drivers with a platform to showcase their skill, style, and innovation. The series strives to grow drifting as a motorsport while staying true to its grassroots origins, ensuring that every event delivers excitement across millions of viewers.
